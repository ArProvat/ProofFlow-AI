# Day 2 visual review

Review performed on eight bundles: CASE-003 (quantity), CASE-007 (missing
information), CASE-008 (match), CASE-020 (partial delivery), CASE-023 and
CASE-030 (invoice total), CASE-028 (price), and CASE-029 (supplier). Checked
the invoice, PO, and receipt in each bundle for legible values, table
alignment, wrapped descriptions, header order, and distance between the
item table and totals. CASE-007's absent invoice number has a visible empty
label and no value or evidence entry. Its other visible values retain
evidence. CASE-020 bills exactly what was received, below the order amount.
CASE-030 prints an invoice total seven dollars above its subtotal and zero
tax.

PDF text and the annotated evidence boxes are also checked automatically for
all 90 originals. The six JPEG derivatives are intentionally degraded with
small rotation, reduced contrast, slight blur, and JPEG compression. Source
PDF coordinates are invalid for them. No external OCR baseline has yet been
measured on the scans.
